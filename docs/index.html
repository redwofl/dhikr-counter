Dhikr Counter

Privacy Policy — Last updated: September 28, 2026

Overview

Dhikr Counter (“the app”) is a dhikr and tasbih counting app developed by redwofl (the “developer”). This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your dhikr data never leaves your device.

Data That Stays on Your Device

All of the following is stored only in your device’s local app storage. It is never uploaded to the developer or any server, and it is removed when you uninstall the app:

  • Dhikr counts, sessions, rounds, and daily/weekly progress
  • Custom dhikr templates you create
  • Settings (language, theme, sound, daily goal, bead style)
  • Saved prayer-time location, if you choose to save one

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, and no analytics of its own.

Location

The app requests your precise or coarse location only to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction for your position. Location is used on-device, is optional (the app works fully without it — you can enter coordinates manually), and is never transmitted off your device by the app. You can revoke location permission at any time in Android Settings.

Ads

The app displays ads served by Google AdMob (Google LLC), which keeps the app free. Ad providers may use your device’s advertising identifier and approximate location data (derived from your device settings, not your GPS position in the app) to serve and measure ads, as described in Google’s Advertising privacy policy.

To limit ad personalization, open Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or opt out of ads personalization on older versions).

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Reminder content is generated on-device; nothing is sent from a server. You can disable reminders in the app or revoke the notification permission at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. Because all user data stays on-device and the app contains no user accounts, no such data is collected from any user.

Deleting Your Data

Because everything is stored locally, uninstalling the app permanently erases all of your data — counts, templates, and settings. If you believe your data was handled in a way that conflicts with this policy, contact the developer using the details below and you will receive a response.

Changes to This Policy

Updates to the app may change how it works, and this policy will be updated accordingly with a new “last updated” date. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
